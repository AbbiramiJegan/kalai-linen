Sustainability

Made With Consideration

We work with mills and makers who've practiced their craft for generations, choosing fibre for its source, its feel, and how it ages — not for a certification sticker.

Small-batch production, thoughtful packaging and construction built to be repaired, not replaced. What we're improving is as important to us as what we've already done.

Our Story

Born in the Tropics

KALAI began with a simple idea: what we wear in warm climates should feel as good as it looks. Rooted in Malaysia and shaped by a long relationship with tailoring and textiles, we create clothing for a slower, more considered way of dressing.

We don't believe sustainability is a slogan. We believe it's a practice — asking better questions about what we make, how we make it, and how long it can remain useful.
