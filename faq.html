About KALAI

What we make, and who it's for

Natural, linen-led clothing for warm-climate living — shirts, trousers, dresses, co-ords and tailoring for both women and men. Everything we make is designed to be worn often, not just looked at. Start with The Kalai Edit.

Fit & Fabric

Sizing, weight and how it wears

Yes — that's what it's built for. Our linen sits between 130–170 GSM, lightweight and open-woven for airflow. Every product page lists the exact fabric weight, feel and the occasions it's best suited to, so you're not guessing from a photo. Read more in Linen vs Cotton: Which Is Cooler?

Every product page includes garment measurements in both cm and inches, the model's height and the size they're wearing, and a plain-English fit description — relaxed, tailored or true-to-size. We don't rely on a generic S/M/L chart alone.

Quality & Sustainability

What you're actually buying

We photograph every piece the same way — front, back, close-up and in movement — under consistent lighting, so colour and drape are accurate. If a colour still reads differently on your screen than in person, our returns policy covers that.

Check the fabric composition on the product page, not just the photo. We list the exact material, weight and construction detail for every piece, alongside genuine customer reviews. You can also read about our approach on Materials & Makers.

We try not to use blanket claims like "eco-friendly." Our Sustainability page names the actual materials, the makers we work with, and what we're still improving — including what we haven't solved yet.

Shipping & Returns

Delivery, duties, exchanges

Use the country selector in the header to see delivery estimates, duties and currency for your location before you check out — not after.

Exchanges and returns are straightforward within 30 days of delivery, and the policy is shown right next to Add to Bag — not buried on a separate page you have to go looking for.

Payment & Checkout

Keeping checkout simple and secure

Yes. We use recognisable, secure payment providers, and checkout asks only for the information needed to complete your order — no account required unless you want one.
